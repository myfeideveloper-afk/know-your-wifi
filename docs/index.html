How this works

Your browser tries to connect to every address on your network. Devices that are switched on answer within about a second, and empty addresses don’t answer at all. It then waits about 20 seconds and checks again, because computers sometimes give a false “answer” for empty addresses. Nothing is installed and nothing leaves your network.

  • Chrome / Edge may ask to “look for and connect to devices on your local network”. Click Allow.
  • The type is a best guess from which ports a device keeps open. Macs (AirPlay) and devices with their own web page (routers, printers) can be told apart. Phones and smart-home gadgets look the same, so they share a group.
  • Browsers can’t read device names, MAC addresses or Wi‑Fi network names (SSIDs). Click a device to give it a nickname.
  • Windows PCs, iPhones and watches usually block every connection, so they stay invisible and aren’t counted. Treat the number as a minimum.
  • Only scan networks you own or are allowed to scan.
